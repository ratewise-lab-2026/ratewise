Why this site exists

Marketplace fees are deliberately confusing: a percentage here, a fixed fee there, an ad fee that only applies sometimes. A seller can make 50 sales a month and still not know whether they are profitable.

These tools answer one question honestly: after every fee, what is left? They are free because the site is supported by a small affiliate commission when you choose to try a tool linked from an article — never by selling your data. See the affiliate disclosure.
