About RateWise

RateWise is a small independent site with one job: show sellers the number that actually matters — profit after fees.

It is run by one person, not a company. The calculators are free because the site earns a small commission when a reader chooses to try a tool linked from an article. That is the whole business model, and it is why there are no pop-ups, no email gate and no “sign up to see your result”.

How the numbers are kept honest

  • Every fee figure comes from the platform’s own pricing or seller-help pages, and is dated on the page.
  • Every calculator lets you override the rate — platforms change fees, and your dashboard is the final authority.
  • Where a page contains an affiliate link, it says so at the link and on the disclosure page.

What this site is not

It is not tax, legal or financial advice. It does arithmetic on numbers you give it. For anything that affects your taxes or your business structure, talk to a qualified professional in your country.
