Etsy Fees 2026: the complete breakdown

Every fee Etsy charges a US seller in 2026, in one place, with the real-world example that shows how much of a $35 order actually reaches you.

FeeRateCharged on
Listing fee$0.20 Every listing, every 4 months, and again on renewal
Transaction fee6.5% Item price + shipping + gift wrap

Off-site ads: the one that decides your margin

Sellers making under $10,000 per year can opt out of off-site ads. Sellers above that threshold are in the program permanently. When a sale is attributed to an off-site ad, Etsy takes 15% of the item price plus shipping — the single largest fee on most orders. Sellers above the threshold pay 12% instead.

You do not choose which sales get the fee; Etsy attributes them. That means your yearly ad cost is partly luck, and it is why you should model the worst case (15%) rather than the average case.

Three fees most sellers forget

  • Shipping is not fee-free. The transaction fee and processing fee both apply to the shipping amount the buyer pays. If you charge exact postage, you still lose a percentage of it.
  • Currency conversion. Selling in a currency other than your payout currency adds a conversion cost on top of the listed fees — it does not show up as an Etsy fee line, but it reduces your deposit.
  • Auto-renew. A listing renews every 4 months (and relists after each sale) at $0.20. With 100 live listings that is $20 every 4 months whether or not anything sells.

How to keep more of each sale

  1. Price on net, not gross. Decide the profit you want, then solve for price — the calculator's “price for a 30% margin” does this for you.
  2. Raise price instead of eating the ad fee. A $5 price increase on a $35 item more than covers the 15% off-site fee on that order.
  3. Keep a fee log. Etsy changes fees and the regulatory fee has moved more than once. One table with the date you last checked keeps your pricing honest.
