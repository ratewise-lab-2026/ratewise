Etsy vs Shopify: which fees actually cost less?

Etsy looks expensive per sale. Shopify looks cheap per sale but charges every month whether you sell anything or not. Here is the actual crossover point.

The two fee structures

CostEtsyShopify (Basic)
Monthly platform fee$0~$39/month
Per-sale marketplace fee6.5% transaction 0% on your own store
Payment processing3% + $0.25 ~2.9% + $0.30
Listing fee$0.20 per listing / 4 months$0
Built-in buyer trafficYes — this is what you are paying forNo — you bring every visitor
Ad costsOff-site ads 15%/12%; Etsy Ads optional You buy ads yourself (Meta, Google, TikTok)

The crossover, in numbers

Take a $35 item with $5.99 shipping (revenue $40.99). Assume the same 3% ad spend on both platforms and no off-site ad attribution.

  • Etsy: 6.5% transaction on $40.99 ($2.66) + processing $1.48 + listing $0.20 ≈ $4.34 per sale.
  • Shopify: processing $1.49 per sale + $39/month fixed. The $39 is recovered after about 13 sales per month; below that, Shopify costs you more than it saves.

So the question is not “which is cheaper” but “can I bring myself more than ~13 sales a month?” If yes, Shopify’s per-sale economics win and you keep the customer relationship. If no, Etsy’s zero fixed cost is the rational choice — and the 6.5% is the price of Etsy’s traffic.

The hidden cost of leaving Etsy

The fee table ignores the thing Etsy actually sells: buyers. On Shopify you pay $39/month and you must generate every visitor. For a beginner, a realistic ad cost to acquire a first-time buyer is $10–$30 — often more than Etsy’s entire fee on a $35 item.

The winning configuration for most small sellers is not either/or:

  • Etsy first to learn what sells and to get reviews.
  • Shopify later for repeat buyers, bundles and custom orders, at the point where you already have ~20+ sales/month.

Practical decisions this changes

  1. Under ~13 sales/month: stay on Etsy, price for the fees, do not pay for a storefront.
  2. Around 20+ sales/month with repeat customers: add a store, keep Etsy as an acquisition channel.
  3. On either platform: price from the calculator’s net-profit number, not from the listing price.
  4. On Etsy: model the 15% off-site ad worst case — it can turn a healthy margin into a loss.
