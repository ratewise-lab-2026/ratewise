Privacy policy

Short version: the calculators run in your browser. Your numbers are never sent to a server.

What we do not collect

  • No account, no email required to use any calculator.
  • No calculator input is transmitted anywhere — the maths happens in your browser.
  • No selling of personal data, ever.

What is collected

Standard web-server logs (IP address, page requested, browser type) are kept by the hosting provider for security and debugging. If analytics or advertising is added in future, this page will say so before it goes live, and it will use a provider that respects regional consent rules.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies of its own. Third-party links (for example to a marketplace or a tool) may set cookies on their own sites under their own policies.

Children

This site is a general-audience tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
