Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the seller pays this site a commission at no extra cost to you. That is how the calculators stay free.

Which links are affiliate links

Links to third-party products — for example store platforms, seller tools and software — may be affiliate links. They are marked with a short note near the link. Links to marketplace help pages and pricing pages used as sources are not affiliate links.

What this does not change

  • The price you pay is the same.
  • Recommendations are based on the fee maths, not on which program pays more.
  • If a tool is not worth the money for a small seller, the page says so even when an affiliate program exists.

Programs

This site may participate in affiliate programs operated by marketplace and software companies. Program terms and commission rates change; the maths on any page does not depend on them.
